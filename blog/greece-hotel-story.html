Greece Hotel Story

Published on February 8, 2025

When I bought my apartment in Albania, I took a trip to Greece with some friends to buy some furniture. They have an IKEA and overall better quality goods. We spent the night in a cool small town. We were looking for a hotel online, but knowing that we were booking a hotel same day, I said, "Hey let's just go into places and try to bargain, it's last minute."

We go to this old cool mid-century hotel. An old guy at the counter offers €60 for a 3-person room which is actually a killer deal for such a cool hotel, but I counter €40 naturally.

Firm no.

"Okay, but what if I come back tonight and the room is still available?" Firm no again, nothing he can do.

"But no one will take the room if it's available tonight, it's €40 or zero!"

We go back and forth for a bit until he says, "Listen. The price is the price, €60. If you're looking for a cheap room, go around the block to a place called xyz, it's cheap there."

I tell him I understand and wish him a good day, thank him for the suggestion.

We go to a couple more spots with no luck; no rooms or they won't bargain.

We go back to the cool mid-century hotel sheepishly. "Okay, we'll take the room, €60."

He takes my passport, I fill out some paperwork and take out 2 €50 bills.

"You don't have €10?" (to make 60) I shake my head. He shrugs and takes one of the €50 bills. "Okay, no problem. I like you."

It never hurts to ask!
