Belgrade Bar Story

Published on July 18, 2024

Whenever I go to a new country I like to find cool bars that specialize in making their own drink recipes.

I came to a bar called "Riddle." The door was locked, I had to knock. A bouncer comes out, slurring-his-words drunk. He ushers me in enthusiastically and shakes my hand.

I get to the bar and ask for a menu. The bartender says, "We don't have a menu, just tell me what flavors you want and I'll make something good."

"Uhm, okay, spicy and fruity."

The guy starts doing theatrics, throwing shakers up in the air.

He calls for ice, and another bartender throws it from across the bar. The guy catches it with his bare hand, still shaking the shaker in the other. Which was extremely impressive because I forgot to mention he's also drunk. Well, drunker than your average bartender.

He splits the shaker, starts pouring from a mile high, clearly spilling like half the drink. "Enjoy, my friend!"

It was very good.
